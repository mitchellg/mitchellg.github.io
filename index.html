    • I'm a computer science PhD student at Stanford University in the SNAP group, advised by Professor Jure Leskovec. I've been an intern at Google (3x) and at CMU. I completed my B.S. in computer science at the University of Rochester in 2016.

      My research interests are (very) broadly defined as building and understanding systems that enable novel interactions with computers, people, and their environments.
